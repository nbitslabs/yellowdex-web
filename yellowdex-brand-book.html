01 — Mission & personality

Make on-chain context feel obvious.

Yellowdex turns walls of anonymous Ethereum-style addresses into labeled context wherever you browse. Local-first. Quietly powerful. Built for people who care enough to read the addresses.

Tone

Calm & precise.

We don't shout. We resolve ambiguity. Short, declarative, written like an indexer log that became a sentence.

Posture

Power user energy.

We assume the reader knows what an address is. We respect their time. No 101 hand-holding unless asked.

Promise

Private by default.

Labels are local-first. Sync, export, and sharing are user-controlled. Every visual choice should reinforce that quiet trust.

02 — Logo

The rounded Y mark.

A label sticker, a highlighter swipe, a token of attention. Use the bundled public/logo-yellowdex.svg mark, served by the site as /logo-yellowdex.svg, on paper, card white, or charcoal. Never place it on competing yellows or busy photography.

Paper — preferred

Card white — secondary

Charcoal — high contrast

Clearspace

Always leave at least 1× the mark's height of breathing room on all sides. Never crop, rotate, or skew.

Minimum size

Don't render the mark below 20×20px on screen. The production header and footer use 40×40px.

05 — Voice & tone

Write like an indexer that learned manners.

Plain words. Active voice. No hype. We earn trust by being correct first and clever second.

✓ Do
  • Lead with what it does. "Label every address." Not "Revolutionize your on-chain experience."
  • Short sentences. Five words good. Ten words fine. Twenty words? Cut it.
  • Concrete examples. Show a:0x4a3b…, not "advanced search syntax."
  • Quiet confidence. Yellowdex doesn't need to convince — it just shows.
  • Respect privacy in every sentence. Local-first. You control sync, exports, and sharing.
✗ Don't
  • No buzzwords. Avoid: revolutionary, seamless, game-changing, unlock, leverage, synergy.
  • No emoji-stuffed headlines. One emoji as punctuation, max. Never as a substitute for a verb.
  • No "we built this for you." Just describe what it is.
  • No fake urgency. No "Don't miss out!" or "Limited time!" The product earns attention by being useful.
  • No address shaming. We don't mock degens. Yellowdex helps everyone read better, including ourselves.

Sample headlines

"Label every address. Turn chaos into context."

"Built for power labelers."

"Dex-level context in three steps."

"Local-first labels; you control sharing."

06 — UI elements

Components, examples.

Buttons

Install on Chrome -> Install on Chrome See how it works Header/hero CTA: charcoal bg + sun text. Final install CTA: sun bg + charcoal text. Secondary: white or transparent surface.

Chips & tags

Beta v0.1.0 Privacy-first ● local-only Rounded 999px. Tight padding. Body font for labels; system mono only for code-like values.

Cards

Card surface

Card white at 90% opacity

Tailwind rounded-3xl, border-black/5, bg-card/90, and shadow-amber/10 keep cards soft on paper.

Hover behavior

Lift +2px, not +10px

Restraint. Yellowdex isn't a game UI. Subtle elevation signals interactivity without being theatrical.

Address treatment

When showing an address inline, use system mono + a soft accent chip. Truncate as 0x4a3b...c0ff unless the full hash is critical.
